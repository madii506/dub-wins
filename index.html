    08 — Fair play

    Fair play, in big letters

    Stats public

    We read your wins from your public career stats. Private stats can't be read, so they can't be paid.

    Battle royale only

    Solo, Duos, Trios and Squads count. Limited-time modes and Creative don't.

    10 paid dubs a day

    Each account gets paid for up to 10 dubs per day. Every dub still counts toward the dev crown.

    Hold some $DUB

    Your payout wallet needs 50,000 $DUB at payday. No hold, no payout that day.

    One you, one wallet

    One wallet per Epic account and one Epic account per wallet. Proven with the privacy switch.

    The day is UTC

    Days run 00:00 to 24:00 UTC. A dub counts on the day our tracker sees it.

    No impossible wins

    A Victory Royale takes time. Jumps faster than a match can be played aren't paid.

    No dubs, no payday

    If nobody wins on a day, that day's pot rolls into the next one.

    09 — FAQ

    Questions

    How do you know I won?

    Fortnite keeps public career stats for every account. Every few minutes we read your battle royale wins through Fortnite-API. When the number goes up, that's a dub.

    Why switch my stats off and on?

    It proves the account is yours without asking for your login. Only the owner can flip that switch. Without it, anyone could claim a pro player's wins.

    When do I get paid?

    The day closes at 00:00 UTC. The payout list is fixed then, and each payout is sent from the creator wallet. When it lands, it shows here with a link to the transaction.

    How much is a dub worth?

    It depends on the day: the creator fees $DUB earned that day, split over every paid dub. The dev takes 25% first. Try the numbers in The split.

    What is "the dev"?

    The player with the most dubs that day. Ties go to whoever got there first. The dev takes 25% of the pot on top of their dubs, and the crown on the board.

    Do wins from before I registered count?

    No. We note your win count the moment you finish registering. Only Victory Royales after that count.

    I play duos with a friend. Who gets the dub?

    Both of you, if you're both registered. Every registered player whose stats show the win gets their own dub.

    What's a creator fee?

    On pump.fun every trade of a coin pays a small fee to the coin's creator wallet. For $DUB, those fees are the pot. We read the vault on-chain, so the pot on this page is the real number.

    Can I register more than one account?

    One wallet per Epic account and one Epic account per wallet. Every account has to pass the privacy switch, so you can't claim someone else's.

    What if my stats go private later?

    We can't read private stats, so nothing is counted while they're off. Switch them back on and we pick up again. Wins from the dark stretch only count as fast as matches could really be played.

    How fast do dubs show up?

    Usually within a few minutes. Fortnite updates your career stats after each match, and we read active players every couple of minutes.

    Is DUB made by Epic Games?

    No. DUB is an independent fan project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games. Fortnite is a trademark of Epic Games.
