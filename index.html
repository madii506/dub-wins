      09 The watchtower

      Fair play

      The tower sees everything. These rules are written into the arena and the tracker, so nobody gets paid for a win that didn't happen.

      In the arena

      One run, one life

      Start it and it counts. It ends when you crash, or at 2:00. No pauses, no restarts.

      Same world for both

      Every duel has one secret seed. Both players run the exact same course, block for block.

      Every move is checked

      The arena replays your moves on the server and scores the run itself. A run sent back faster than it can be played scores 0.

      Paid the second it ends

      The winner's 95% goes out in one transaction from the two duel addresses. Ties are refunded.

      Your SOL, your duel

      Each stake sits in its own address that can only pay the winner or refund the player.

      Nobody showed?

      Cancel any time before someone takes your duel. Unclaimed duels refund themselves after a day.

      On the board

      Stats public

      We read wins from public career stats. Private stats can't be read, so they can't be paid.

      Battle royale only

      Solo, Duos, Trios and Squads count. Limited-time modes and Creative don't.

      10 paid dubs a day

      Each account is paid for up to 10 dubs per day. Every dub still counts toward the dev crown.

      Hold some $DUB

      Your payout wallet needs 50,000 $DUB at payday. No hold, no payout that day.

      One you, one wallet

      One wallet per Epic account and one Epic account per wallet, proven with the privacy switch.

      The day is UTC

      Days run 00:00 to 24:00 UTC. A dub counts on the day the tracker sees it.

      No impossible wins

      A Victory Royale takes time. Jumps faster than matches can be played aren't paid.

      No dubs, no payday

      If nobody wins on a day, that day's pot rolls into the next one.

      10 The campfire

      Questions

      How does a duel work?

      You pay your stake and run a blocky world: switch lanes, jump and slide for as long as you can last, grabbing gems on the way. Your score goes up in the lobby. Anyone can take you on for the same stake and run the exact same world. The higher score takes 95% of the pot, paid the moment the second run ends.

      How is a run scored?

      One point for every block you run, plus every gem you grab: gold 5, emerald 12, diamond 30. You speed up the longer you last. The run ends when you crash, or after 2 minutes.

      Where is my SOL while I wait?

      In an address made for your side of that one duel. Only the arena can move what's in it, and only two ways: to the winner, or back to you. You can watch it on Solscan the whole time.

      What if nobody takes my duel?

      Cancel it from the lobby any time before someone pays in, and your SOL comes back minus the 0.000005 SOL network fee. Duels nobody takes refund themselves after a day.

      What stops cheaters?

      Your score isn't sent, your moves are. The arena replays every move in the duel's secret world and works out the score itself. A run sent back faster than it could be played, or one that took far longer than it could, scores 0.

      Where does the 5% go?

      To the creator wallet of $DUB, in the same transaction that pays the winner. A tie pays no fee: both players get their stake back.

      How do you know I won?

      Fortnite keeps public career stats for every account. Every few minutes we read your battle royale wins through Fortnite-API. When the number goes up, that's a dub.

      Why switch my stats off and on?

      It proves the account is yours without asking for your login. Only the owner can flip that switch. Without it, anyone could claim a pro player's wins.

      When do I get paid?

      The day closes at 00:00 UTC. The payout list is fixed then, and each payout is sent from the creator wallet. When it lands, it shows here with a link to the transaction.

      How much is a dub worth?

      It depends on the day: the creator fees $DUB earned that day, split over every paid dub. The dev takes 25% first. Try the numbers in The split.

      Do wins from before I registered count?

      No. We note your win count the moment you finish registering. Only Victory Royales after that count.

      I play duos with a friend. Who gets the dub?

      Both of you, if you're both registered. Every registered player whose stats show the win gets their own dub.

      What's a creator fee?

      On pump.fun every trade of a coin pays a small fee to the coin's creator vault. For $DUB, those fees are the pot. We read the vault on-chain, so the pot on this page is the real number.

      Can I register more than one account?

      One wallet per Epic account and one Epic account per wallet. Every account has to pass the privacy switch, so you can't claim someone else's.

      What if my stats go private later?

      We can't read private stats, so nothing is counted while they're off. Switch them back on and we pick up again. Wins from the dark stretch only count as fast as matches could really be played.

      How fast do dubs show up?

      Usually within a few minutes. Fortnite updates your career stats after each match, and we read active players every couple of minutes.
